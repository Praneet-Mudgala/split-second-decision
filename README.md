# Split Second Decision

Split Second Decision is a fast paced puzzle platformer built in Godot 4.5.1 for Hack Club Terra Week 4, following the theme "Split"

Play as a sentient vanilla ice cream scoop and a cherry sharing a body that both need to escape a banana split before it is served and they are eaten. You must play quickly as you swap forms, break blocks, and jump to escape. Play as fast as you can and try not to die, as your time and death count are being tracked. Try and get the best score you can in the four levels!

Play in this link in your browser!: https://praneet-mudgala.github.io/split-second-decision/

## Controls and Mechanics

### Controls
A/Left Arrow and D/Right Arrow: Move left and right
E: Swap forms between the ice cream and cherry strategically, even in midair
Q: Smash attack that breaks fragile objects; Only the ice cream can do this
W/Up Arrow: Jump; Only the cherry can do this
R: Quick restart for the current level
P: Pause menu 

### Mechanics
Ice Cream: The ice cream is slow and heavy and cannot jump, but he can break through fragile chocolate blocks by pressing Q. Use his attack and his fast falling to escape!
Cherry: The cherry is fast and light and can jump, and he can also use the marshmallow jump pads to jump even higher. Use his fast speed and mobility to escape!

## Running Locally
If you want to look at the source code or run the game locally:
1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open Godot Engine 4.5.1 and import the project.godot file
3. Press f5 to run the game and explore the code!
